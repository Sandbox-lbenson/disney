Shades of Green (SOG) — Is it worth considering?

SOG 1–2 day Disney tickets are ~10% off pre‑tax and tax‑free. That’s effectively ~15–16% vs buying direct with 6.5% tax. Using your $600 all‑in ticket estimate: pre‑tax ≈ $563.38 → SOG ≈ $507.04, saving $92.96. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
