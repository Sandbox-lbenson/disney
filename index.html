Disney Trip Cost Sheet

Dates not fixed. Hotel is a shuttle property around $563 pre-tax. All numbers are estimates using current public pricing.

Inputs used

Tickets shown are for 5 days in early Oct 2026 from the Disney site screens.
Airport costs to PIT: gas $40, parking $13 per day. Parking is calculated for 6 days = $78.
Bus from MCO: LYNX Link 311 at $2 per person per ride.
Hotel taxes 12.5 percent. Typical resort fee $36 per night + 12.5 percent = $40.50 per night.
Drive option: 2,200 miles round trip, 22 mpg, gas $3.04 per gallon, road hotel $120 each direction, food on the road $60 per travel day.
Food during stay in Orlando set at $100 per day.
